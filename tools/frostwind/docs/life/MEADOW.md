# Meadow and dream-life dossier

The meadow traveller is the same persistent person as the boreal and swamp
traveller; scene-specific appearance does not create a second identity.

Colossus, strider, and pilgrim are unique persistent fictional species. Their
body authority is `src/machine.js` and `docs/MACHINES.md`. They are not animals:
no hunger, reproduction, mammalian emotion, pair bond, or herd behavior is
implied.

## The stork and the hare

Two real animals, no invented law. Both are pose tables on `src/skeleton.js`.

The white stork (*Ciconia ciconia*) is here for the cut sward. Storks walk
behind mowers and stock and take what the disturbance flushes, and this
steading cuts hay. One adult sits the nest on the pole at the north end of
the steading (`makeNestPole`: a cartwheel on a 6 m post, a real practice). The
other stands on one leg at its foot, stalks the sward, and follows a walker
3 m back. Its display is the bill clatter with the head thrown back. The
meadow declares no white, so the body takes `loam`. Flight, the brood, the
change-over at the nest and migration are not claimed.

The brown hare (*Lepus europaeus*) is here for open, quiet grass. It has no
burrow. By day it lies in a form with its ears flat and sits tight; at the
pinned noon that is its whole behaviour, until a walker flushes it. Leverets,
boxing, home range and density are not claimed.

## Fictional law

The curvature-heart may change the rest curvature of contiguous bronze.
Gravity, mass, momentum, support, contact, and elastic response remain ordinary.
Life behavior is therefore authored fictional law constrained by firm
substrate, thermal fields, route traces, support, and pressure consequences.

## Individual traditions

- **Colossus** surveys and settles broad open sites.
- **Strider** follows and reinforces long route traditions.
- **Pilgrim** attends the court and other authored anchors.

Coordination is stigmergic: compacted routes, pressure prints, familiar anchors,
and authored shared sites can couple actions without assigning human motives.
Each machine persists as one named record across unloading and returns with the
same route, commitment, pressure trace, and appearance.

The traveller encounter is a small authored tradition: one nearby machine may
approach, pulse its normally pastel-red eye pastel green three times while the
eye shape and the rest of its body stay unchanged, return the eye to red,
accompany the traveller briefly at its normal slow gait, and then resume its
suspended route. There is no speech, dialogue state, pursuit beyond the
encounter radius, command relation, or claim that the signal expresses human
friendship.

Thermal and substrate rows are explicit fictional/derived resource
relationships. They must affect placement, action choice, disturbance, traces,
or diagnostics; decorative tables are invalid.

Non-claims: consciousness model, mammal affect, ecology-by-metaphor, continuous
off-screen footfalls, or machine population dynamics.
