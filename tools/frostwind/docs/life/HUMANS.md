# Human life and culture dossier

Human identity is authored through habits, obligations, attachments, witnessed
facts, and one concrete unfinished project. There are no personality, hope,
friendship, anxiety, or emotion scores. A hope is a project with visible
milestones. A relationship is a typed tie with obligations and recognition.

## Current residents

- The **elder** moves among yard hearth, seat, storage, and threshold anchors
  while ordering the winter household.
- The **merchant** travels with one stable load identity, then unpacks,
  displays, attends, and repacks it.
- The **woodcutter** follows a woodlot, splitting-block, and fuel-stack circuit
  that leaves stumps, slash, billets, and a changing reserve.
- The **swamp fisher** tends traps, rack, mooring, and punt.
- The **swamp boatwright** inspects winch, boardwalk, mooring, and water marks
  when place state requires work.
- Fisher and boatwright share a mutual-repair obligation. Kinship and friendship
  are explicit non-claims.
- The **traveller** is one person across scenes. Player action emits observable
  facts and disturbances; the scheduler never controls locomotion.

## Perception and memory

Residents receive partial sight and sound observations gated by range, cover,
motion, and scene state. They remember what they could witness, not player
intent. Routine repetitions collapse. Open commitments, project changes, and
salient person/place events remain in a bounded deterministic set.

Interruption follows commitment rules: orient or withdraw, remember a salient
fact when warranted, then resume the suspended duty. Preparation and attention
are expressions of that semantic transition; they are not a separate mind
simulation.

## Culture compiler

`src/culture.js` defines meanings and practices before binding them to
world-generated use nodes. Nature can reject an impossible package but cannot
derive its symbolism, obligations, ownership, taste, or social categories.
The boreal counterfactual package deliberately shares environmental conditions
with the active package while choosing different meanings, making that
separation executable.

Household and work-group membership never implies kinship. Recognition never
implies friendship. Ownership means a declared use/maintenance obligation, not
a duplicate string on a character sprite row.
