# Current Decisions

Compact ledger of decisions that are easy to re-litigate or mistake for missing
work. Code and tests are authoritative when this ledger drifts.

Status: **active** = current contract; **open** = known work; **deferred** =
deliberately postponed; **concession** = accepted cost or incomplete model.

## World and rendering

| Decision | Reason | Evidence / test | Status / code |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`render` owns the frame; `Scene` owns the world.** | GPU targets, batches, and internal resolution are frame state. Generation, chunks, entities, tracks, places, and visibility are world state. A scene asks the renderer for dimensions rather than caching another authority. | Scene and golden tests exercise outdoor grids. | **active**: `src/render.js`, `src/scene.js` |
| **Scenes are exclusive: one resident bake context.** | The atlas-bake `LIGHT` is a module global. Two resident worlds would make it ambiguous. Portal crossings dispose and rebuild. | Portal/scene tests; explicit guards and disposal paths. | **active**: `src/scene.js`, `src/sprite.js` |
| **Cabins and houses are outdoor props only.** | Doors do not cross into room places. Exterior silhouettes, colliders, and culture home anchors remain. | Scene portals are world portals only (`gen.portals`); no `to:'room'` crossings. | **active**: `src/scene.js`, `src/cabin.js`, `src/steading.js`, `src/swamp-settlement.js` |
| **World transitions are portals, not biome regions.** | Different latitude, light, weather, and palette cannot truthfully crossfade across a short walk. | **Latitude, output palette, aerial treatment, and reflection response are per scene.** | Boreal values repainted other surfaces and a shared palette carried colours the frame never used. | **The meadow's lake field is computed in one place.** | It was written twice — analytically in `naturalLakeAt` and again inlined in `waterMask` — so the drawn shoreline and the collider were two opinions that happened to agree. | **Meadow relief is flattened at `elevAt`, and hills are deferred.** | A hill whose silhouette you cannot see is not a hill, it is a stain on the grass — measured, the terrain normal put 1069 px patches of a second grass rung on open ground. | **The sward's colour noise is held to the amplitude its own spec declares.** | `docs/MEADOW.md` already stated that sward colour noise is ±0.030 and cannot cross a rung boundary, and the code applied the snowfield's ±0.115 drift field on top — 69% of a rung, drawn but never declared. | **A building's collider is its footprint, and `built` is a site condition every life reader shares.** | Two dream machines stood on the roofs of two houses, and three things were wrong at once. The wall was three r=13 discs at 30 px pitch — 86 px of a 114–134 px house, 7 px deep — so the outer seventh of every wall and the whole front-to-back crossing were walk-through. `entityAffordance` gated every step on medium and habitat, neither of which knows a wall is there. The disc line this row argued for was itself replaced once buildings grew a depth; see the row below |
| **A declared shell collides with its breadth; the traveller keeps its point test.** | Inflating chunk solids by the traveller's own 2.5 px would change where every world seats it on the first frame — `standNear` walks until `blocked` clears — which is a change to framing rather than to collision, and it moves winter's goldens. The mark is a few pixels of the next rung on the edge that causes the state, then a half checker that washes out. A rim is as complete as its cause: the wet rim is cut from the narrowest capillary band, so every peat waterline carries it, and a first version on low ground only was rejected for its gaps. | **Gusts advect downwind at the mean flow, so sway crosses a stand as a front.** | A gust is a pattern carried by the wind (Taylor 1938). Periods, weights and scatter stay authored; sway stays a one-pixel event. | `test/weather.test.mjs` checks the front speed, the across-wind line and the shared shader function. | **active**: `GUST` in `src/weather.js`, `Scene.resolve` in `src/scene.js` |
| **A world is a plan row; a stage branches on the plan, never on the biome name.** | Sixty-two biome-name branches in one 3,000-line closure made each new world an edit to every part of the generator. | **A position is an identity, so a spell stores one row and nothing is tagged.** | The world is a pure function of seed and position and chunks are evicted and baked again, so an id on a tree would not survive. | **A fire is the disc the impulse lit, swept along one wind run.** | A mark must stay a closed form: eight rows, no grid of burning cells. The impulse lights a disc whose radius pays the bed's heat of pre-ignition, and the bed then runs its head fire downwind for one flame residence, to the first break in the bed. ## Models, authorship, and scale

| Decision | Reason | Evidence / test | Status / code |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Procedural generation supplies texture and valid variation; authoring places structure and meaning.** | Natural law can constrain a landmark but cannot choose its cultural importance or narrative placement. | POIs and portal/steading plans are explicit; vegetation and surfaces are field-driven. | **active**: [VISION.md](../VISION.md), `src/world/`, `src/pond-court.js` |
| **Built architecture is a shared constructive layer with four vernaculars on it.** | `src/construct.js` owns the units: the cabinet volume and its three faces, the course wall, the roof plane with its riser and eave cast, the recessed opening, the bay rhythm. The meadow house, the boreal cabin, the swamp pile house and the lakehouse all take their volume and their roof from it. Each keeps its own cladding, because they genuinely disagree: horizontal log courses, vertical board-and-batten, split shakes. A rhythm that differs between two forms stays with the form; widening a shared emitter waits for a THIRD that disagrees with both, which is the rule `src/gait.js` and `src/quadruped.js` were earned under. | `test/construct.test.mjs` proves the units; `test/architecture.test.mjs` asserts every shell carries depth and renders in `ao`, and prints the share per building. | **encoded**: `src/construct.js` and the four makers |
| **A building was drawn without its depth, and nothing could see it.** | The world's depth axis draws straight up the screen, so a camera-facing wall has a side face zero pixels wide. Every house took that literally: `HOUSE_M.d = 4.5 m` prices at 34 screen rows through `obliqueRows` and `makeHouse` drew 0 of them, 4037 pixels of front face and nothing else. | **A shadow needs the footprint, not just the front edge.** | The projected pass shears a sprite from one horizontal line, so what it sweeps is the building's FRONT EDGE. That last term is depth. It made the hub 1.1 m taller than it is, lengthened the cast, and pushed the root of that cast past the ground the machine stands over. Stated from the height terms alone the root lands correctly, and the shear puts it back up-screen by exactly the setback, because that is where the ground under it is. | `test/steading.test.mjs` measures the hub in METRES against head plus mast, which is the axis the mistake was on. | **encoded**: `houseRotorLift` |
| **A reflection mirrors about the water, not about the sprite.** | `W_REFLECT_VS` took the caster's grounded edge to be its own bottom row, which is true of anything standing on the ground and false of a roof rotor.
