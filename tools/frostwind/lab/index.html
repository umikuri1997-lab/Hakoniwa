the cast

Read out of the baked atlas and out of ACTOR, so a new row in src/fauna.js with a baked bank appears here with no edit to this page. Rigged animals carry their own clip table and are shown at the cadence that table declares, which is the only way a wolf's canter is seen at all: no speed threshold reaches it.

The human cast is the grid-authored person rig: src/body-folk.js owns anatomy and contact, src/figure-folk.js the raster. Judge sheets with node tools/person.mjs.

what this page is not

No ground, no cast shadows, no snowfall, no interior. A shadow is a statement about the ground a body stands on and there is none here; the hearth is zero because this is the outdoor view. Every uniform that affects the sprite itself is at the game's outdoor default, and lab/stage.js names each one it could not reproduce.

The profile view is drawn unflipped. In the game it is mirrored when the animal faces left — a mirror, not a second drawing.
