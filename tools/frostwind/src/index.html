Frostwind / Manifesto

Manifesto

What this is about

Frostwind is a nature-grounded world compiler for coherent, beautiful imaginary pixel worlds.

What is it for?

Frostwind is for making worlds too large to draw by hand and too specific to leave to noise. A creator describes a world at the level of meaning. Frostwind turns that intent into explicit models, generates valid possibilities, and compiles them into deterministic pixel art.

It is a physically grounded world model, not a learned predictive model. It is not a general game engine or a prompt-to-image system. The playable world is its reference implementation.

The rule

Nothing is drawn that can be derived.
Nothing is chosen that can be measured.
Nothing is claimed that cannot be checked.

Derive, measure, assert. “It looks right” is a hypothesis. Where no law exists, choose deliberately, name the choice as taste, and apply it consistently.

Store reasons, not results

The image is the end of the process, not the source of it.

  • A tree is a growth strategy responding to climate, light, and competition.
  • A place is hydrology, footprint, and architecture—not a decorated rectangle.
  • A life is an identity with needs, commitments, relationships, memory, and consequences.
  • A sprite stores material, normal, occlusion, and thickness—not colour.

Shared causes make the world agree with itself. Scale agrees across people, built forms, animals, and terrain. Materials agree with light. Species agree with habitat. Culture responds to nature without being determined by it.

Five kinds of knowledge

  • Nature provides physics, climate, light, water, terrain, disturbance, and decay.
  • Life provides growth, morphology, habitat, movement, ecology, and inheritance.
  • Culture provides settlement, architecture, tools, food, ritual, history, and memory.
  • Craft provides scale, projection, silhouette, palette, animation, and the pixel grid.
  • Beauty provides hierarchy, rhythm, restraint, surprise, and a declared direction.

Nature supplies causes. Life occupies and changes those conditions. Culture turns constraints into meaning. Craft translates reality into the medium. Beauty selects among coherent alternatives.

Equal complete inputs, including state and seed, return equal answers. Terrain comes from analytic fields. Placement is admitted by affordance. Light resolves against material. The final frame is snapped to a closed scene palette.

The same method makes three very different places. Frostwood derives snow and boreal light from a northern climate. Sun Meadow combines open-canopy light and lake geometry with an authored steading and dream-machines. Life, culture, and authorship

Frostwind compiles reasons and consequences everywhere, but bodies only where they can be observed. Off-screen life advances through meaningful events. Nearby bodies are reconstructed from the same identity, commitment, route, and recent consequences.

Environment constrains culture, but does not choose its meanings. The same river, forest, cold, or flood can support many histories. Procedural systems grow continuity and variation. Authors establish identity, landmarks, exceptions, and places that matter.

Terrain and ecology are what one walks through. Authored places are what one walks to.

The LLM boundary

An LLM may research a domain and propose schema-checked parameters: a species grammar, material ramp, body row, place plan, or art-direction profile. Those parameters pass through the same projection, lighting, palette, selection, and tests as hand-authored data. Runtime LLM access is not required.

The model is the guardrail.
The LLM writes tables, not pixels.

What grounding cannot do

Grounding offers no leverage where there is no law. A dream world needs an authored physical authority before it can be derived honestly. Tests protect scale, invariants, palette membership, and deterministic model and sprite synthesis. A GPU frame check confirms that every output colour belongs to the scene palette. Tests cannot prove that a composition is beautiful. Looking still matters.

Nature provides the laws. Life provides growth and interaction. Culture provides meaning. Craft provides the language. Beauty provides direction. Frostwind compiles the world.

Why does a nature-grounded world need dream creatures?

Because grounding is a method, not a demand for realism. Frostwind is meant to make imaginary worlds coherent. The dream-machines test whether it can invent honestly instead of only copying nature.

Each machine begins with one declared fictional law: a curvature-heart may change the rest curvature of contiguous bronze. Gravity, mass, momentum, support, contact, and elastic response remain ordinary. That one departure has consequences for the body plan, gait, balance, habitat, behaviour, and final silhouette.

The fiction is named. Everything that can still be derived is derived. This is the useful boundary: imagination chooses the law; the compiler follows it through.

03Compiler

Meaning goes in. A world comes out.

Equal complete inputs, including state and seed, return equal answers. Between intention and frame, every stage has one job and passes explicit structure to the next.

04Two authorities

Reality is not the only canon.

Pixel art has equally binding limits. A physically correct answer that cannot be read at the project’s scale is not a correct answer for this medium.

Canon A · Laws of the world

What makes the thing true

Physics, biology, anatomy, construction, ecology, use, and culture: area at a branch split, light through a canopy, a chair under a body.

Canon B · Laws of the medium

What makes the thing legible

Integer rows, projection, silhouette, value groups, closed palettes, composition, animation cadence, and explicit floors.

When the canons conflict, the medium wins and the deviation is recorded. A 26-row figure cannot describe a realistic 3.5-row head, so its proportion comes from a measured legibility floor—not an unlabelled nudge by eye.

05Evidence

The picture is downstream of the model.

These are not decorative equations. Their outputs are read by the drawing code, and tests protect the consequences.

Law 01 · Human scale

Law 02 · Projection

screen_y = world_y / 2 − world_z

Law 03 · Canopy light

06Proof worlds

One method. Three different worlds.

Grounding does not make worlds converge. Shared laws make their differences believable.

62° N · Frostwood

Cold light, boreal shelter

Solar geometry and canopy attenuation shape the light. Sky exposure decides where snow can settle. Boreal life and buildings occupy compatible terrain at a common human scale.

Latitude → light → snow → habitat → shelter → pixel

38° N · Sun Meadow

Open light, cultivated place

Analytic lake basins, sward, and palms arise from climate and morphology. A steading and three dream-machines add authored use and meaning that no climate equation could choose.

Basin → shore → sward → work → landmark → pixel

28° N · Green Swamp

Water as road

One hydrology field resolves hummock, flooded floor, shallow light, and deep water. Habitat and travel follow those bands; pile houses and shared wet infrastructure answer them culturally.

Hydrology → habitat → affordance → meaning → pixel

07Living world

Alive beyond the camera.

Frostwind compiles reasons and consequences everywhere, but bodies only where they can be observed. Off-screen life advances through semantic events. Nearby bodies are reconstructed from the same identity, company, commitment, route, appearance, and recent consequences.

A hope is a concrete unfinished project with visible milestones. A relationship is a typed tie with obligations and recognition. A trace is evidence that something happened before the traveller arrived. The world does not become unreal when it leaves the frame; it becomes economical.

Environment constrains valid culture, but does not choose its meanings. The same cold, river, forest, or flood can support many histories. Nature supplies pressures. People author answers.

08Creator + machine

LLMs should make decisions, not images.

A creator stays free at the level of meaning and intention. An LLM can research a domain, interpret that intent, and propose narrow, schema-checked parameters. The compiler makes those proposals face the same laws, projections, palettes, selection, and tests as hand-authored data.

Meaning-level input

  • A species grammar and habitat envelope
  • A material ramp and construction vocabulary
  • A body row, place plan, or daily practice
  • An art-direction profile with declared priorities

Compiler-level guardrails

  • Explicit authorities and bounded schemas
  • Deterministic seeds and measurable consequences
  • Affordance gates and laws of the medium
  • Tests, visual review, and recorded concessions
The model is the guardrail.
The LLM writes tables, not pixels.

09Authorship

Grow variation. Author meaning.

Procedural systems are excellent at texture, continuity, consequence, and valid alternatives. They do not know what a place means, which exception matters, or where attention should rest.

Derive where a model exists. Generate where variation is valuable. Select composition deliberately. Author culture, landmarks, identity, and exceptions that no natural law can choose.

10Limits + direction

Grounded does not mean finished.

Grounding offers no leverage where there is no law. Trees benefit from botany; water from optics; buildings from construction and use. A dream world needs an authored physical authority before it can be derived honestly.

The method cannot replace looking. Tests can protect scale, invariants, palette membership, and byte-identical frames. They cannot prove that a composition is beautiful. Measurement narrows the search. Visual judgment remains authoritative for art direction.

  1. World laws are executable in the terrain, surface, sunlight, life, and culture models.
  2. Medium laws are executable in scale, projection, sprite synthesis, composition, and quantisation.
  3. Claims, sources, scope, downgraded evidence, and refused folklore live in the project’s canon register.
