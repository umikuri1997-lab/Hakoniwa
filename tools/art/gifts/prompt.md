# おくりもののアイコン（13こ）をたのむプロンプト

もちもの・ポップアップ・せいれいの頭の上に出す、アイコンの絵。マップに置く物ではないので、お手本のキューブはつけない。
もらったら `tools/art/gifts/` に入れて、`gifts.js` の `GIFTPIX`（いまはコードの仮の絵）をおきかえる（`ICONS` に `gf_<名前>` で入れる）。

## プロンプト（このまま1つでわたす）

A single sprite sheet of 13 small cute keepsake items for a cozy pastoral life-sim game, arranged in a 4-column grid (4, 4, 4, 1), each item centered in its own cell with plenty of empty space around it, on a fully transparent background. No grid lines, no cell borders, no labels, no text, no shadows on the ground.

Items, in this order (left to right, top to bottom):
1. a smooth, pretty pebble (pale blue-gray, glossy)
2. a clear glass ball (pale aqua, with a soft white highlight)
3. a seashell, fan-shaped scallop (pale pink)
4. a soft white bird feather, slightly curved
5. a pine cone (warm light brown)
6. a pressed flower: a small pink flower pressed flat on a cream paper card
7. a small hand-carved wooden bird figurine (light honey wood), standing upright
8. a wooden spinning top (soft red-orange body, light wood stem), upright
9. a tiny straw doll made of woven grass (pale straw yellow), standing upright, simple round head, no face
10. a picture postcard showing a green landscape scene (pale green and cream)
11. a single glass marble (pale mint green with a swirl inside)
12. a kaleidoscope tube (pale lavender), lying diagonally
13. a small glass bottle with a cork, filled with tiny star-shaped sand (pale sky blue bottle, cream stars)

Do not draw anything that looks like a weapon, armor, jewelry, amulet, charm or food. Upright items (the wooden bird, the spinning top, the straw doll) stand perfectly vertical, not tilted.

Style: soft hand-painted game icon style, the same gentle storybook look as a cozy farming game. Pale, light, soft colors; no dark or heavily saturated colors. Clean flat colors with soft shading; no noise, no grain, no speckles, no dithering, no gritty texture. No thick or dark outlines; if an outline is used, it is a thin 1-pixel line only slightly darker than the color next to it, never black or near-black. Light comes from the upper left. Each item is drawn large and clear so it reads well when shrunk down.
